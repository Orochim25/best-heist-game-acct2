# NIGHT LEDGER — Five Heists in the City of Tarn

You are **Rook Ashford**, the thief the Tarn underworld only knows as ROOK.
Your handler Ivo the Broker has lined up five jobs across the city, one night,
one ledger to settle: steal the prize in each, slip out through the green
hatch, and never let a cone of lamplight settle on you.

**5 heists, 18 guards, 33 treasures, 4 gadgets.**

1. **The Harbormaster's Office** — steal *The Tide Ledger* (2 guards, par 75s)
2. **The Velvet Meridian Casino** — steal *The Meridian Chip* (3 guards, par 95s)
3. **The Tarn Mint** — steal *The Sovereign Plate* (4 guards + camera, par 120s)
4. **The Halcyon Gallery** — steal *The Widow's Pearl* (4 guards + cameras + laser, par 140s)
5. **The Obsidian Vault** — steal *The Nightingale Diamond* (5 guards + cameras + lasers, par 160s)

## How to play

- **Move** WASD / arrow keys. **Sneak** hold C or Ctrl (slow, near-silent).
  **Sprint** hold Shift (fast, loud). Walk is the middle gait.
- **Gadgets** — press 1–4 to pick, Q or left-click to use at the cursor:
  1. **Coin** — thrown, rings out and pulls a guard off their post.
  2. **Smoke pellet** — a cloud no cone can see through.
  3. **Lantern douse** — snuff the nearest lamp for 20 seconds.
  4. **Noisemaker cricket** — winds up and ticks, drawing guards away.
- **Light is the rule**: lit floor exposes you, shadow hides you, tall grass
  hides you unless a guard is right on top of you. Crates and pillars block
  sight and movement.
- **Guards** patrol named routes, pause to look around, and run a suspicion
  meter: patrol → suspicious → investigate → chase → search → return.
  Cameras sweep their own cones; lasers blink on and off — cross only when dark.
- **Alarm**: get caught three times in one heist and the job is blown.
  Grab the named prize, then the green hatch opens — escape to finish.
  Rank per heist: GHOST, SHADOW, CUTPURSE or RUCKUS.
- **M** mute, **P** / **Esc** pause, **R** restart a heist from the results screen.

## Play it

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network —
every file the game loads lives under `game/`.

`?autopilot=1` runs all five heists with the built-in pilot and reports the
result; `?shot=cover|spotted|lure|win` stages a screenshot scene from the
real simulation.
